### 🌊 [AquaStrike](https://github.com/wilymanhoward/AquaStrike) *(Multiplayer 3D Arena Shooter)*
> **Tech**: Unity, Photon PUN 2, C#, URP, Procedural Audio & Decal VFX  
- **Multiplayer Architecture**: Designed real-time 1v1 matchmaking with 5-character private room codes, public lobby browser, and live synchronized in-game chat.
- **Fluid Locomotion**: Engineered dynamic slide mechanics (`11 m/s`), first-person camera lerping, and physics bouncers (`0.65` elasticity).
- **Combat & Weapons**: Built pressurized water ammo reservoirs, 3 distinct blasters with recoil, and trajectory-predicted water bombs via real-time `LineRenderer`.
